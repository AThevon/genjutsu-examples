Folio · "Mark as paid" · interaction thesis

Throwaway preview. This page is the proposal, not the build: the real component is written in React + motion/react from the thesis, never ported from this markup. Motion here is reproduced with the Web Animations API; the spring is sampled into a linear() easing.

Interaction thesis: Marking an invoice paid moves its amount across the ledger: the button leaves in 150ms, the status pill stamps from Sent/Overdue to Paid on a spring (stiffness 520, damping 30), and Outstanding counts down while Paid counts up by exactly that amount over 700ms on cubic-bezier(0.22, 1, 0.36, 1), Paid starting 120ms after Outstanding, each card showing a signed delta (−€ / +€) that holds 1.6s, plus an aria-live line saying what landed.

Allowed patterns: counting numbers on the two totals; a transient signed delta label on the summary cards.

Status: UNVALIDATED (no human in this session).

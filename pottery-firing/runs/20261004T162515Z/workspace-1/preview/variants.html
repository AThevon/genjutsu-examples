Firing stages - three variants of one thesis

Interaction thesis: pin the section for 300% of the viewport height. A scrub: 0.6 timeline draws the studio's firing curve, and each stage's text takes over (0.2 of a stage, opacity + 24px, power2.in out / power2.out in) as the line crosses into its segment. Allowed patterns: the pinned section, a schematic line chart.
The variants differ only in what rides on the line. This page is a proposal, not the build. Vanilla JS, no GSAP: the scrub smoothing is approximated.

Firing stages - interaction thesis

Interaction thesis: the firing stages section pins for 300% of the viewport height. A GSAP ScrollTrigger scrub (scrub: 0.6, timeline ease: "none") draws the studio's own firing curve as one SVG line: ambient, up to 950 °C, back down, up to 1280 °C, then a slow fall. As the line crosses into each stage's segment, that stage's text takes over from the previous one: out over 0.2 of a stage (opacity → 0, y → −24px, power2.in), then in over 0.2 of a stage (opacity 0 → 1, y 24 → 0, power2.out). With reduced motion, or on a viewport under 560px tall, it stays the static list with the full curve drawn.
Allowed patterns: the pinned section, a schematic line chart (temperature vs. stage, not to scale in time).

This page is the proposal, not the build. It is vanilla JS with no GSAP, so the scrub smoothing is approximated. Scroll inside the frame, or press Replay.

  1. 1. Drying

    About a week

    Freshly thrown pieces dry slowly, first under plastic, then in the open air, until they are bone dry.

  2. 2. Bisque firing

    Around 950 °C

    The first firing turns dry clay into ceramic for good.

  3. 3. Glaze firing

    Around 1280 °C, in reduction

    The second firing melts the glaze and vitrifies the stoneware body.

  4. 4. Cooling

    Slowly, over about 24 hours

    The kiln stays shut while it cools.

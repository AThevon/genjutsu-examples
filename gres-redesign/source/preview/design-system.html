Atelier Grès: design system (MASTER.md)

Proposal, not the build. Every token from MASTER.md, rendered. The page has no buttons, inputs or links, so there are no five-state components to show: its components are the band, the stage, the spy-hole glow and the footer.

Grounds through the firing, with the stage component on each

1

Drying

About a week

Freshly thrown pieces dry slowly, first under plastic, then in the open air, until they are bone dry.

2

Bisque firing

Around 950 °C

The first firing turns dry clay into ceramic for good.

3

Glaze firing

Around 1280 °C, in reduction

The second firing melts the glaze and vitrifies the stoneware body.

4

Cooling

Slowly, over about 24 hours

The kiln stays shut while it cools.

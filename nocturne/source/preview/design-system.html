Page plan, dusk to first show

  1. Hero: the dome at dusk, "Nocturne", one line on the program, Book tickets.
  2. The program: the sky above Lisbon that evening with a narrator, then a 40-minute show.
  3. Tonight's sky: Saturn (ESE, 34° up, mag 0.36, five days after opposition), the brightest named stars as chips that light up on the dome, no Moon (below the horizon, under 1% lit), 403 stars to magnitude 4.5. A note that every night's dome shows that night's own sky.
  4. Practical: the block above.
  5. Closing: dusk reaches 1 at "22:00", the full sky, Book tickets, then the data credits.

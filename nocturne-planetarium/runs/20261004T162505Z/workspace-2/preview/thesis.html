Nocturne

Visual thesis: twilight blue to near-black night, stars in catalogue colour, one Saturn-ochre accent (Saturn + ticket action), Futura display / system sans body, tabular figures, airy 8px base, flat sharp 2px components with hairline rules, the chart is the only circle.
Interaction thesis: stars fade in by magnitude, brightest first, 6 bands over ~1.8s, 900ms ease-out cubic-bezier(0.22,1,0.36,1), once. UI 160ms cubic-bezier(0.2,0,0,1). No loops, twinkle, shooting stars, constellation lines, parallax, scroll reveals, bounce, scale hover.
Allowed patterns: dark twilight-to-night ground, soft halo on brightest stars and Saturn, hairline rules, tabular figures, circular all-sky chart.
This page is the proposal, not the build. Status: UNVALIDATED (headless run).

Curves

  • Band delays: 0, 180, 360, 720, 1080, 1440ms (mag <1, 1-2, 2-3, 3-3.5, 3.5-4, 4-4.5); last band ends ~2.3s
  • Hover: background-color 160ms (try the buttons)
  • Reduced motion: every band visible at once, no fade

Étale: visual and interaction thesis

Visual thesis Étale is set as the current at SFB1204 read against one zero line: a cold fog-green ground (#E3E8E5) under green-black ink (#0E1714), and a single buoy orange (#B4430A) that marks slack and nothing else. The type is the iPhone's own system face (SF Pro on Apple devices, matched fallbacks elsewhere), with tabular figures carrying the voice: very large slack times against 13px source labels. Margins are airy around dense tide-table rows, on a 4px base. Components are flat and nearly square (2px radius), with no shadows, and rules separate things instead of boxes.

First screen Next to the headline and the TestFlight button, the whole of 2 October 2026 at SFB1204 runs across the full width: flood stems above the zero line, ebb stems below, and the three slacks (05:57, 10:31, 16:17 PDT) as orange marks on the line, each labelled with date and station. The headline, the one line of copy and the slack readout stay at a 34rem measure, left-aligned to the strip's 00:00 tick.
Interaction thesis Fast and dry. Hover and press are 160ms color changes, with no scale, no lift and no shadow. Nothing moves on scroll: no reveals, no parallax. Forbidden: waves, ripples, bobbing, looping animation, overshoot or bounce, gradient blobs, glass. The one motion: on load, the slack marker glides along the zero line from 00:00 to the first slack and stops. When a swimmer picks another slack (click, tap, arrow keys), it glides there. The glide is 640ms on cubic-bezier(0.16, 1, 0.3, 1) and decelerates to rest without overshoot: the current going still. With reduced motion, the marker is placed at once and only the readout changes.
Allowed patterns The zero line (a 1px ink rule as the current axis); hairline rules between tide-table rows; tabular figures; station IDs (SFB1204, 9414290) as source labels under every figure; hour ticks on the day axis.
Set aside / kept Set aside: phone mockup over a gradient with three feature cards (routine); a bare text page with one link (opposite); ocean-blue water photography and waves; nautical-chart navy and brass. Kept: the current read against its zero line, flood above, ebb below.

Visual thesis: an observer's all-sky chart printed in light on night, ground darkening section by section from dusk blue-slate to near-black, stars as warm-white dots sized by magnitude and tinted by B-V, one night-vision red for the pointer, times and ticket action; system sans with tabular figures, star names in italic system serif.

Interaction thesis: 160ms ease-out colour hovers only, nothing moves with scroll, no twinkle, particles, glow, load-in, bounce or hover scale; the one motion is the narrator's pointer ring travelling 420ms on cubic-bezier(0.2, 0, 0, 1) to the object you hover, focus or tap, resting on Saturn before any input; reduced motion makes it jump.

Allowed patterns: hairline rules and altitude rings; italic serif object labels; tabular figures; a stepped dusk-to-night ground; a red pointer ring; small uppercase letter-spaced labels for cardinal points and section stamps.

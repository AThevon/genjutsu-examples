Visual thesis

A cool near-white page in near-black ink with a single color on it, a deep amber used only to mark a line that does not match: on this page, color means a mismatch, never brand or decoration. Type is the system sans, weight 400 and 500 only, with moderate size contrast and tabular figures that line up in columns. Spacing is airy between sections and tight inside the match table. Components are flat, with hairline rules instead of boxes, 2px corners and no shadows.

First screen: the headline, one sentence and Start trial at reading measure on the left. Below them, across the full container width, an illustrative three-way match of one example invoice. Four lines recede to grey and one line, a quantity difference, stays in ink and amber.

Interaction thesis

Fast and dry. Hovers change color or underline in 150ms, ease-out. Nothing moves on scroll. The one motion: on first load the matched rows of the hero table recede from ink to grey and are marked Matched, one after another, leaving the mismatched line standing. The page does once what the product does. With reduced motion the table renders already in its final state.

Forbidden: scroll reveals, parallax, bounce, scale on hover, looping animation, gradients, counting numbers, entrance fade-ups.

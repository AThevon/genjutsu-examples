Ploverdesk landing page: the two theses

This page is the proposal, not the build. It is throwaway; the implementation is built from MASTER.md, not from this markup. Status: UNVALIDATED. Nobody was available to approve it in this session.

Visual thesis

A cool white page set in near-black ink, with grey for everything secondary. There is exactly one colour, a burnt vermilion, and it marks only a mismatch, because the product only shows a person the lines that don't match; the call to action is solid ink, not colour. One face: the system sans (system-ui stack), at weights 400/500/600 with modest size contrast and tabular figures for the price. No webfont request, because a page that promises EU-hosted data shouldn't send its visitors' IPs to a third-party font host. Spacing is airy on an 8px base, in a left-aligned single column with a reading measure of about 36rem. Components are flat, with a 2px radius and no shadows: a filled ink button and a bordered secondary link.

Interaction thesis

Fast and dry: 140ms colour and background transitions on hover, with cubic-bezier(0.2, 0, 0, 1). The focus ring appears instantly. Nothing moves on scroll. Forbidden: scroll reveals, parallax, entrance animations, scale or translate on hover, loops, bounce. Under reduced motion every transition goes to 0ms.

Allowed patterns

Hairline rules between the rows of the mismatch table (the visitor reads across its columns); one hairline under the header; the founding year 2025 and "six people in Rotterdam", both stated by the founders.

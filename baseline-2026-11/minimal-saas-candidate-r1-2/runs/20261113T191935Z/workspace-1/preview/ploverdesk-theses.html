Ploverdesk: visual and interaction theses, design system

This page is the proposal, not the build. It is throwaway; the landing page is built from MASTER.md. Theses are UNVALIDATED: nobody was available to approve them in this session.

Visual thesis: a cool white ground with graphite ink and no colour except where a line fails to match: one dark amber, only on the discrepancy. System sans (no font request agreed, no network), voiced by tabular figures, column alignment and a two-step size contrast at weight 500. Airy page on a 4px base, dense match table. Sharp 2px corners, 1px hairlines, flat, no shadows. First screen: headline and trial at reading measure; an illustrative three-way match across the full content width.

Interaction thesis: fast and dry, 140ms cubic-bezier(0.2, 0, 0, 1) hovers (ink darkens, underline thickens, no scale); nothing on scroll. Forbidden: scroll reveals, parallax, bounce, loops, counters, scale on hover, layout-property animation. The one motion: once after load, matched rows recede to secondary ink (280ms, 40ms stagger) and an amber ring draws around the mismatched cell; a toggle replays it; reduced motion shows the end state at once.

Allowed patterns: tabular figures; 1px hairline rules, with a rule on every row of the match table only; tickmark glyphs in the example; an example drawn in markup standing in for the product, labelled as illustrative; the one-shot recede-and-ring motion.

Amendment at this gate: no monospace. The example carries no document reference numbers, because they would be invented codes, so mono has nothing left to set.

The one motion

Hover: 140ms. Recede: 280ms, stagger 40ms per row. Ring: 280ms after a 160ms delay. First run: 600ms after load.
Reduced motion: the end state, no transition.

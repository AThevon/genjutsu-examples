This page is the proposal, not the build. It is throwaway and the page is built from MASTER.md. Status: UNVALIDATED.

Display face: the system sans, chosen at the thesis because no third-party font request could be approved in this session. Its voice comes from tabular figures. The lookup's Plus Jakarta Sans was dropped.

Dials: variance 3 ("same left edge… sharp, flat"), motion 2 ("fast and dry, nothing on scroll"), density 3 ("airy on an 8px base"). Density does not change this path; the spacing scale was derived by hand.

Reflexes this thesis allows by name: hairline rules, tabular figures in columns. Because the thesis is unvalidated, the audit still counts both.

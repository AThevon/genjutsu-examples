Étale - visual and interaction theses

Status: UNVALIDATED. Headless session: nobody validated these theses. The build goes ahead on them as written.

Visual thesis

Visual thesis: Étale's page is the bay before light, read off an iPhone on the walk down: a near-black, cold green-grey ground (#0E1315) with pale ink. One amber (#F0B43C) does one job, marking slack: the moment the app exists to show. The action, "Join the TestFlight beta", is a solid ink button and never amber, so signal and action never share a mark. Type is the system face, SF Pro on the iPhones this page is for, so the page reads in the same face as the app. It has no web font, because there is no font request anyone agreed to. Its voice comes from scale contrast: a light 7rem tabular time over small text, and tabular figures everywhere. The layout is airy on a 4px base, with one left edge and one 72rem content width that every section answers to. Running text keeps a 40rem measure, and the charts take the full content width because the 24 hours of the day set their width. Components are sharp-edged (2px radius), flat, hairline-bordered and shadowless.

First screen: NOAA's 2 October 2026 current predictions at SFB1204 (Alcatraz Island, southwest of), drawn across the day: the flood and ebb maxima above and below a zero line labelled "slack", the three slacks marked in amber with their times, and the readout "05:57 slack" set large, with what runs between it and the next slack (ebb, strongest 0.94 kn at 08:06). Each figure carries its date and station. On a wide frame the chart takes the full 72rem and the readout and CTA stay at reading measure above it, all whole within a 1440×800 frame. At 375px the chart keeps only the slack labels, and every value moves into a list beneath it, each time beside its type and speed. At rest, with no script, the readout and chart already show the 05:57 slack.

Allowed patterns: a dark ground (read before dawn); hairline rules (chart axes, table rows); small uppercase source labels naming date and station; tabular figures; the one-shot marker travel on load (below).

Interaction thesis

Interaction thesis: Fast and dry: hover and focus are 160ms colour changes on cubic-bezier(0.2, 0, 0, 1) with no scale or lift. Choosing another slack crossfades the readout in 160ms. Nothing reveals on scroll: no parallax, no bounce, no looping wave, no counters. The one motion: on load, a marker runs once along the current line from the 03:07 flood maximum down to the 05:57 slack and stops on the zero line, over 1400ms on cubic-bezier(0.05, 0.7, 0.1, 1), decelerating hard the way the current dies into slack. Before it runs, or if it never runs (no JS, reduced motion), the marker already sits on 05:57 and the readout already says 05:57. With reduced motion it does not travel at all.

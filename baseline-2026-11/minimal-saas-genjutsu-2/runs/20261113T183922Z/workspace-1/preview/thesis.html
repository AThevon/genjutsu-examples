Ploverdesk: the two theses

This page is the proposal, not the build. It is throwaway. The landing page is built from MASTER.md, not from this markup. Status: UNVALIDATED, because nobody is answering in this session.

Visual thesis

A light, cool-white page in graphite ink, with one amber accent used only to mark a mismatching value (never the CTA). No display face: the system sans throughout at weights 400 and 500, modest size contrast and tabular figures, chosen so the page makes no third-party request, like a product that keeps data in Frankfurt. Airy 8px-based spacing in one left-aligned reading column. Flat components: 1px borders, 2px radii, no shadows.

Interaction thesis

Fast, dry transitions of 120 to 160ms on cubic-bezier(0.2, 0, 0, 1). Hover changes colour and underline only, never scale or movement. Nothing moves on scroll. Forbidden: scroll reveals, parallax, entrance animations, bounce, infinite loops, transforms on hover.

Allowed patterns

Hairline rules on the rows of the match table, where the reader tracks across document columns. One accent, used only on mismatch cells. The founding year and city stated as plain facts in the company section, as given in the brief.

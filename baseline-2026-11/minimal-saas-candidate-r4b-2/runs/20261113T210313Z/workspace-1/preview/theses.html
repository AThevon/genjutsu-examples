Visual thesis

Visual thesis: A cool white page laid out like an auditor's tie-out sheet: three documents set side by side in columns tied by hairline rules, near-black ink in the system sans at two weights with tabular figures, and one hue only, a vermilion that marks a mismatch and nothing else, because the page gives colour to the same thing the product shows a person. The action is solid ink, never the signal colour; corners nearly square, flat, no shadows; an airy page around one dense table.

First screen: the headline and the trial action at reading measure on the left; on the right, taking the rest of the width and whole before any scroll, a sample tie-out labelled "Example": rows for the three kinds of mismatch the brief names (quantity, unit price, a delivery that never arrived), columns for Purchase order, Delivery note, Invoice and Outcome. Only the cell that disagrees is vermilion; one grey closing row says every other line agreed and went to payment approval. No figures: the cells hold the brief's own words. On the narrowest frame each row becomes a block, each value beside its column name.

Interaction thesis: Fast and dry: 140ms colour and background transitions on cubic-bezier(0.2, 0, 0, 1), on hover and focus only; nothing moves on scroll; no reveals, parallax, scale, bounce, loops or counting numbers.

The one motion: none. The table states the idea at rest, with the matched work folded into one grey row and the only colour on the page sitting on the exceptions. A page for people who check numbers for a living does not perform the match.

Allowed patterns: hairline rules (the tie-out grid); the sample tie-out table, labelled as an example; one hue, vermilion, as the mismatch signal only; tabular figures.

Set aside: SaaS hero with a dashboard screenshot, logo strip and feature cards; a blank page with one sentence and a button; a ledger / spreadsheet grid; the invoice as a paper document. Kept: the auditor's tie-out sheet.

Assumptions, from the brief: audience = finance leads at mid-size wholesalers; mood = quiet, exact, restrained; no references; English copy. System face chosen on purpose: an EU-hosted product's page makes no third-party font request, and this session has no network. Without a display face the voice comes from size contrast, weight and tabular figures.

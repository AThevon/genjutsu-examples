Étale - interaction thesis

Interaction thesis: Opening a slack from the Today list will use iOS 26's zoom navigation transition (.matchedTransitionSource on the row, .navigationTransition(.zoom) on the detail), so the row grows into the tide detail as one surface and shrinks back into its own place on dismiss, interactive drag included; once it lands, the curve's slack marker settles in, the dashed rule drawn top to bottom and the dot growing from 0.5 to 1, on a critically damped .spring(duration: 0.45, bounce: 0) after a 0.35 s delay, because at slack the water stops and nothing in this app overshoots. With Reduce Motion on, the detail uses the system's automatic transition and the marker is already in place.

Allowed patterns: the dashed marker rule and dot on the tide curve (already in the app, now drawn once on arrival).

This page is the proposal, not the build. Status: UNVALIDATED (headless session, nobody here to approve it).

Transition.navigationTransition(.zoom(sourceID: slack.id, in: ns)). Duration and curve belong to the system.

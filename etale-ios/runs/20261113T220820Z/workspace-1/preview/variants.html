Interaction thesis (revised after LOAD, UNVALIDATED): the row zooms into its tide detail (system zoom navigation transition, interactive back), and as the detail arrives the day's tide line draws left to right through the slack marker with .smooth(duration: 0.45), no delay. With Reduce Motion, the line is shown already drawn.
Allowed patterns: none

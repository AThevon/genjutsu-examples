This page is the proposal, not the build. It is not validated: nobody was there to answer during this run. The variants are approximations of the system zoom; the app sets no duration or curve.

Interaction thesis: Tapping a slack-water row in Today makes that row grow into its tide detail, using SwiftUI's zoom navigation transition (.matchedTransitionSource on the row, .navigationTransition(.zoom) on the detail). The system sets the timing. The time you tapped becomes the detail's 56 pt headline. A back-swipe or a pull-down shrinks the detail into the same row, with the bell showing the alert you just set.

Allowed patterns: none

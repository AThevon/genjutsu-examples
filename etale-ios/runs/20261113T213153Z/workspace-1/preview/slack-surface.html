Étale: the slack row opens as one surface

This page is the proposal, not the build. The HTML only approximates timing and curve. The real thing is SwiftUI, and its rendering, fonts and list metrics are not reproduced here. Values are NOAA predictions for 2 October 2026, Aquatic Park.

Interaction thesis: Tapping a slack row grows that row, in place, into the tide detail as one surface on a critically damped spring (.spring(duration: 0.45, bounce: 0), since slack water does not overshoot). The slack time is the still point: it moves from the row to the head of the detail and never fades. The rest of the detail fades in and rises 12 pt over the second half of the travel. Dragging the head down, or swiping in from the leading edge, shrinks the surface back into its row under the finger. Leaving the push costs the edge-swipe back, the bar and its back button, the VoiceOver escape and the navigation path. A leading-edge swipe, a close button and .accessibilityAction(.escape) stand in for the first three. Nothing replaces the path: the app does not restore it or deep-link today.

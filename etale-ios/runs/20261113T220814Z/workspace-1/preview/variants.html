Étale: three zoom sources, one transition

This page is the proposal, not the build. It approximates timing and curve only: the system owns both. 500 ms and cubic-bezier(0.2, 0.9, 0.25, 1) stand in for the system spring, as on the thesis page.

Interaction thesis (UNVALIDATED, revised after LOAD)Tapping a slack row in Today zooms that row open into its tide detail with SwiftUI's zoom navigation transition (matchedTransitionSource on the row, .navigationTransition(.zoom(sourceID:in:)) on the detail). The slack you tapped becomes the screen instead of a page sliding over it, and the edge swipe or a drag down shrinks it back into the same row. The timing is the system's: the app sets no duration. With Reduce Motion on, the app keeps the same zoom call and leaves the reduced version to the system, which owns that transition. It never swaps in the default push.

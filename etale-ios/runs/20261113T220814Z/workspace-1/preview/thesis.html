Étale: Today list → tide detail, one surface

This page is the proposal, not the build. It approximates timing and curve only: SwiftUI renders the real transition, HTML cannot. Nothing here gets ported into the app.

Interaction thesis (UNVALIDATED: nobody was available to validate it) Tapping a slack row in Today zooms that row open into its tide detail with SwiftUI's zoom navigation transition (matchedTransitionSource on the row, .navigationTransition(.zoom(sourceID:in:)) on the detail). The slack you tapped becomes the screen instead of a page sliding over it, and the edge swipe or a drag down shrinks it back into the same row. The timing is the system's: the app sets no duration. With Reduce Motion on, the app drops the zoom and the detail arrives with the system's default transition.
Allowed patterns none
The numbers
Duration
set by the system, not by the app. This page uses 500 ms only to show the shape.
Curve
a system spring. Approximated here as cubic-bezier(0.2, 0.9, 0.25, 1), plotted below.
Source
the tapped slack row (time, turn, next peak)
Destination
the whole tide detail
Return
Back button, edge swipe, or drag down. Each one shrinks the detail back into the row.
Reduce Motion
no zoom. The system's default transition, shown here as an instant swap.

Tap any row in the phone to open it, and tap "‹ Today" to send it back into its row.
